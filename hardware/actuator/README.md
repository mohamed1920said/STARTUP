# AMR 50 mm Quarter-Turn Valve Actuator

This directory contains a prototype mechanical design for a 50 mm PVC ball-valve actuator. The gearbox is reusable for a future 63 mm valve; only the lower clamp and measured stem adapter change.

## Release status

Revision `A-PROTOTYPE` is suitable for fit checks, bench testing, and controlled field prototypes. It is **not released for unattended irrigation or sale** until every hold point in `RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` is closed and the completed actuator passes torque, ingress, electrical, and endurance tests.

The real 5840-31ZY motor and PVC valve were not supplied for measurement. Their interfaces are intentionally marked `HOLD - MEASURE` in the native SolidWorks custom properties and documentation.

## Delivered CAD

- `cad/native/`: 27 manufactured `.SLDPRT` files, purchased-component reference parts, and the resolved 35-component `.SLDASM`.
- `cad/step/`: individual STEP parts and the complete placed STEP assembly.
- `cad/stl/`: printable gears, sensor carriers, trays, and enclosure components.
- `cad/dxf/`: laser/CNC profiles for plates, brackets, and the 50/63 mm clamp variants.
- `cad/renders/`: assembled and exploded verification views plus gear-profile SVGs.
- `cad/motion/`: the verified 6-second open/close GIF, poster, and validation report. Raw viewport frames are deleted after encoding.
- `bom/`: mechanical, material/electrical, and fastener BOMs.

The placed neutral assembly contains 35 components in the 50 mm configuration (the alternative 63 mm clamp is delivered separately). `tools/validate_neutral_assembly.py` performs exact solid-intersection checks and writes `manufacturing/ASSEMBLY_GEOMETRY_VALIDATION.json`. The individual manufactured parts are native feature-based SolidWorks files. Gear bodies use exact 20-degree involute STEP solids generated by `tools/generate_neutral_cad.py` and imported into native parts because the installed SolidWorks 2026 sketch API was unstable on high-segment gear profiles.

The native assembly contains a Basic Motion study named `AMR_Valve_Open_Close`. It drives the 40T motor gear through 180 degrees and the output shaft group through 90 degrees over a 6-second oscillating cycle. The study uses 10 rotary motor features so the output gear, shaft, clutch stack, valve adapter, magnet carrier, reed cam, and manual override remain synchronized. The cover is present in the saved assembly and is suppressed only in the GIF preview.

## Mechanical architecture

The 20 mm shaft carries a four-bolt lower flange. A replaceable stem adapter bolts to that flange, so a different stem profile or valve size does not require a new gearbox. Two 16 mm aluminium bearing carriers provide real 6204 seats and internal retainers; the 6 mm structural plates only locate and support the carriers.

The output gear runs on a metal sleeve around the hub and transmits torque through two friction faces. M16 Belleville preload is adjusted until the clutch opens the measured valve reliably and slips before the weakest protected component. The steel M8 stops sit beyond the electronic targets and hard-limit switches.

The requested 19 mm external manual hex cannot physically surround a 20 mm shaft. The manufacturable design uses a 30 mm external hex, 20.2 mm bore, and cross-pin. Engaging manual mode must open a hardware interlock that disables both BTS7960 enable lines.

## Position and safety sequence

1. AS5600 target at calibrated 0 or 90 degrees.
2. Independent reed confirmation near 2 and 88 degrees.
3. Normally-closed hard-limit switch at approximately 0 and 90 degrees.
4. Adjustable steel stop at approximately -1 and 91 degrees.
5. Friction clutch slip.

Hard limits and the manual-mode switch must disable motor-driver control in hardware; software monitoring is additional protection, not the only protection.

## Electronics compatibility warning

The repository's current `src/actuator/main.cpp` drives a short-pulse latching valve through a TB6612FNG. It is **not compatible** with this continuous 12 V 5840-31ZY/BTS7960 actuator. Do not connect this motor to the current firmware. The required I/O and interlock architecture is documented in `INTERFACE_CONTROL.md`.

## Rebuilding

1. Install SolidWorks 2026 with the standard part and assembly templates.
2. Install Python 3.12 and `cadquery==2.6.1`.
3. Run `python tools/generate_neutral_cad.py` to create exact gears, DXFs, neutral assembly, and renders.
4. Run `python tools/validate_neutral_assembly.py`; release only when unintended intersections are zero.
5. Run `cscript //nologo tools/generate_solidworks.vbs <project-root> parts-only`.
6. Run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/build_motion_study.ps1`; this builds the direct native assembly, creates the Basic Motion study, closes and reopens SolidWorks to validate it, captures the cover-off viewport frames, and encodes the GIF preview.
7. Confirm `cad/motion/AMR_Valve_Open_Close_REPORT.txt` ends with `RESULT=PASS`, reports 35 components, 10 motion features after calculation, a 6-second duration, and gear-centre translation below 0.01 mm.
8. Regenerate the drawing pack with `python tools/generate_documentation.py`.
